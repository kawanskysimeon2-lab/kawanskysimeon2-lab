### about

```
I started coding on a phone. No PC, no gaming setup, no excuse.

A good part of what I build still comes straight from Android, through Termux —
while some people wait for the "ideal computer" to get started, I simply started
with what I had in my hands. Today I also use a Chromebook from school,
and I'm saving up for my own MacBook. Until then, I keep building.

I don't come from an expensive course, a top university, or a network of contacts.
I come from a small screen, a touch keyboard, and stubbornness.

Every script I write is proof, not a promise.
I don't need to convince anyone I'll go far —
the track record will speak for itself.

I'm not the one who talks about what he's going to do.
I'm the one who shows up once it's already done.
```
